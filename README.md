# CodeAlpha_FinancialDashboard

# Financial Health Dashboard — SME Analytics

## CodeAlpha Power BI Internship — Task 1



### Overview

An interactive Power BI Financial Health Dashboard analyzing

revenue, profit, COGS, and sales trends for SMEs using the

Microsoft Financials Dataset with built-in forecasting.



### Dashboard Visuals

- KPI Cards: Revenue, Profit, COGS, Profit Margin,

  Avg Monthly Sales, Segments

- Line Chart: Revenue vs Profit vs COGS Trend + Forecast

- Donut Chart: Profit by Business Segment

- Bar Chart: Revenue by Product

- Column Chart: Profit Margin by Country

- Scatter Plot: COGS vs Revenue Analysis

- Column Chart: Monthly Sales Performance

- Column Chart: Discount Band vs Profit Impact

- Slicers: Year, Segment, Country, Product



### DAX Measures

- Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total COGS,

  Profit Margin %, Avg Monthly Sales, Total Discounts



### Tools Used

Power BI Desktop | Power Query | DAX | Analytics Forecasting



### Dataset

Microsoft Financials Sample — Kaggle

700 rows | 16 columns | 2013-2014 | 5 segments | 5 countries



### Key Insights

- Revenue: $118.7M | Profit: $16.9M | Margin: 14.2%

- Paseo is the top product at $33M revenue

- High discounts reduce profit by 90%

- USA has highest profit margin among all countries
